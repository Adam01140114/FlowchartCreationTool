Employee

What is your last name (family name)?

Enter your legal last name as it appears on official documents.

What is your first name (given name)?

Enter your legal first name as it appears on official documents.

What is your middle initial?

Enter your middle initial if you have one. Leave blank if not applicable.

Have you used any other last names?

Choose Yes if you have used any other last names, such as a maiden name or previous legal name.

What other last names have you used?

Enter any other last names you have used, such as a maiden name or previous legal name.

What is your home address?

Enter your current home address, including street, apartment number (if any), city, state, and ZIP code.

What is your date of birth?

Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

What is your U.S. Social Security number?

Enter your nine-digit U.S. Social Security number.

What is your email address?

Enter your current email address.

What is your telephone number?

Enter your current telephone number, including area code.

Please select your citizenship or immigration status

Select the option that best describes your current citizenship or immigration status. Only one option may be selected.

If you are a lawful permanent resident, what is your USCIS or A-Number?

Enter your USCIS number or A-Number if you selected 'A lawful permanent resident' as your status.

If you are an alien authorized to work, what is your expiration date (if any)?

Enter the expiration date of your work authorization if you selected 'An alien authorized to work.' Leave blank if not applicable.

If you are an alien authorized to work, provide one of the following numbers

Enter your USCIS A-Number, Form I-94 Admission Number, or Foreign Passport Number and Country of Issuance if you selected 'An alien authorized to work.'

Please sign to certify the information above is correct

Sign your name to certify that the information you provided is true and correct.

What is the date of the employee's signature?

This field will be automatically filled with today's date.

Employer

What List A identity and employment authorization document(s) are you presenting?

List A documents establish both identity and employment authorization (for example a U.S. passport or permanent resident card). Enter the document title, issuing authority, number, and expiration for each document you present. Leave unused rows blank.

What List B identity document are you presenting?

List B documents establish identity only (for example a driver's license). Enter the document title, issuing authority, number, and expiration. Use List B together with a List C document when you are not presenting a List A document.

What List C employment authorization document are you presenting?

List C documents establish employment authorization only (for example a Social Security card). Enter the document title, issuing authority, number, and expiration. Use List C together with a List B document when you are not presenting a List A document.

Is there any additional information the employer should record about the documents examined?

Use this for notes the employer needs to keep with Section 2, such as additional document details or clarifications. Leave blank if none.

Did you use an alternative procedure authorized by DHS to examine documents?

Check this box if you used an alternative procedure authorized by the Department of Homeland Security to examine documents.

What is the employee's first day of employment?

Enter the employee's first day of employment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

What is the name and title of the employer or authorized representative?

Enter the last name, first name, and title of the employer or authorized representative completing this section.

Signature of employer or authorized representative

Sign to certify that you have reviewed the employee's documents and completed this section.

What is the date of the employer's signature?

This field will be automatically filled with today's date.

What is the business or organization name?

Enter the full name of the employer's business or organization.

What is the business or organization address?

Enter the complete address, including city, state, and ZIP code, of the employer's business or organization.

Preparer/Translator

Did a preparer or translator assist you in completing Section 1?

Choose Yes if someone helped you complete Section 1. If Yes, provide their information below.

What is the employee's name as shown in Section 1 for the preparer or translator certification?

Provide the employee's last name, first name, and middle initial as shown in Section 1.

Who helped prepare or translate Section 1 for the employee?

If a preparer and/or translator assisted with Section 1, enter their name, address, and signature information. You may record up to four preparers or translators. Skip this if the employee completed Section 1 alone.

What is the first preparer or translator's information?

Enter the name, address, and signature for the first preparer or translator.

Reverification & Rehire

What is the employee's name as shown in Section 1 for Supplement B?

Provide the employee's last name, first name, and middle initial as shown in Section 1.

What are the details for the first reverification or rehire?

Complete this section if the employee is being rehired or requires reverification. Enter the date of rehire, new name (if applicable), and document information.

For the first Supplement B entry, did you use an alternative procedure authorized by DHS?

Check this box if you used an alternative procedure authorized by the Department of Homeland Security to examine documents for this reverification or rehire.
